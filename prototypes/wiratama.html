Preventive routine

  • Daily: check backup job, production PCs, CCTV recording.
  • Weekly: router/switch config backup, antivirus definitions, log review.
  • Monthly: hardware clean-up, patching window, review open tickets and repeat issues.
1 · VLAN segmentation

Separate broadcast domains: a fault or a guest device in one VLAN does not reach production.

2 · UTP cable termination (T568B)

Same standard on both ends for a straight-through patch cable; test every run before handover.

3 · Rack & patch panel

Labelled ports (room / outlet ID) shorten troubleshooting.

4 · Backup 3-2-1

3 copies, 2 media, 1 off-site — nightly job, monthly restore test.
